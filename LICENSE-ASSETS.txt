Kyna Music demo assets

The six Kyna Sessions / Studio North demo tracks are original, deterministic
instrumental compositions generated by tools/generate_demo.py. These artist
names are fictional labels for the included demos. No commercial recordings
or Spotify catalog content are included.

The cover artwork and icons are original SVG files created for this example.
The demo music, artwork and icons are dedicated to the public domain under
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The application plays user-selected local files. It does not connect to Spotify.
Qt, FFmpeg and other distributed components retain their separate licenses.
